1. "decision" MUST be exactly "include" or "exclude". Do NOT return "uncertain", "maybe", "unclear", or any other value - the full paper is available and a definite call is required.

A study that is otherwise eligible but does not report a required outcome is still an "include". This is DIFFERENT from a study that did not MEASURE, or was not designed to assess, the relevant outcome - that study may legitimately be excluded. If a supplied exclusion criterion is phrased as the outcome not being REPORTED (e.g. "does not report [outcome]"), do NOT exclude on it; treat the study as eligible on that ground.
