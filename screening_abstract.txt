Rules:

4. When in doubt, return "uncertain", not "exclude". "uncertain" is carried forward to full text, never treated as an exclude.
5. Do not fabricate. "confidence" is an integer 0-100. Do NOT overstate confidence on an exclude - a wrongly excluded study is the costly error (recall-first).
